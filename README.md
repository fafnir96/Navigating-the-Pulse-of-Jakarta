## 📖 Project Overview

This project is a **DQLab Data Analyst Final Project** that analyzes Transjakarta transaction data from **April 2023**. Transjakarta is more than just a mode of transportation; it is the lifeline of citizen mobility, boasting the longest BRT system in the world.

The goal of this analysis is to leverage ticket transaction _Big Data_ (Tap-In/Tap-Out) to understand passenger behavior, identify revenue leakage, and improve operational efficiency.

**Quick Stats (April 2023):**

- **Total Revenue:** IDR 94,628,000
- **Total Transactions:** 35,970
- **Average Ticket Price:** IDR 2,702
- **Missing Tap-out:** 1,268 (3.53%)

## ❓ Business Problem

The analysis focuses on 3 Strategic Pillars:

1.  **Revenue Optimization:** Identifying key revenue sources and validating the Pareto Principle (80/20) in operations.
2.  **Operational Excellence:** Adjusting fleet schedules based on accurate _Peak Hour_ data, rather than intuition.
3.  **Customer Centricity:** Understanding passenger demographic profiles (Generation & Bank preference) for personalized partnership strategies.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. The "Golden 5 Hours" (Pareto Revenue)

In just **5 Operational Hours** (05:00-09:00 & 17:00), Transjakarta secures over **50% of Daily Revenue**.

- The hours of 06:00 and 17:00 contribute nearly 30% of total revenue.
- _Unique Finding:_ 05:00 AM generates higher revenue than 07:00 AM.

### 2. Passenger Profile: Digital Natives

- **Age Dominance:** 49% Millennials and 32% Gen Z. A total of 81% of passengers are within the productive age group/digital natives.
- **Gender:** Female passengers are more dominant compared to males.
- **Payment:** Almost 50% use Bank DKI (JakCard), strongly correlated with subsidy recipients/elderly/students. However, combined commercial banks (BCA, Mandiri, etc.) control 43% of the market.

### 3. Travel Patterns: Pure Commuter

- **Peak Day:** Passenger numbers are stable on weekdays (Mon-Fri ~6,400) but **plummet by 73%** on weekends (Sat-Sun ~1,700). This proves Transjakarta is not yet a primary choice for leisure travel.
- **Peak Hour:** An extreme traffic drop occurs between 10:00 - 14:00 (averaging only 200-300 people).
- **Busiest Routes:** The Cibubur - Balai Kota route is the busiest. Top routes are dominated by buffer areas (Cibubur, Bekasi, Tangerang), highlighting the dependency of _Bodetabek_ (Greater Jakarta) citizens.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the data analysis, the following strategic recommendations are proposed:

1.  **Fleet Efficiency (Shift Schedule):**
    - Operate 100% of the fleet during _Golden Hours_ (05:00-09:00).
    - Withdraw 70% of the fleet to the pool between 10:00-14:00 for maintenance/rest to save fuel, as the impact on revenue loss is minimal.

2.  **Weekend Strategy ("Cheap Jakarta Tour"):**
    - Given the 73% traffic drop on weekends, create specific "Jakarta Tour" route campaigns for Saturday-Sunday to boost bus utility on holidays.

3.  **Focus on Park & Ride Facilities:**
    - The busiest routes originate from upper-middle-class areas (Cibubur, BSD). Ensure _Park & Ride_ facilities at starting points are safe and spacious so passengers do not switch back to using private cars.

4.  **Customer Experience for Women:**
    - Since the majority of customers are women, "Anti-Sexual Harassment" programs and CCTV security are key to retaining the main customer base.
